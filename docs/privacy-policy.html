BudT Privacy Policy

Effective 3 November 2026

In short
  • BudT has no accounts and doesn’t collect your personal or financial data.
  • Everything you enter stays in a database on your device.
  • There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking.
  • The only thing BudT sends over the internet is a request for official exchange rates, which contains currency codes and nothing about you.

Who we are

BudT is a personal budgeting app for iOS and Android, developed by Olarotimi Adamson (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what happens to information when you use the app.

Information stored on your device

BudT stores what you enter only on your device. This includes:

  • transactions
  • categories
  • budgets
  • savings goals
  • bills
  • your settings, such as country, currency and theme

We don’t operate servers that receive this data, and we can’t see, access or recover it.

The tax, pension, loan and savings calculators run entirely on your device. Nothing you type into them is saved or sent anywhere.

Information we collect

None. BudT doesn’t include analytics, advertising, crash-reporting or tracking software, and it doesn’t ask for an account, email address or phone number.

Exchange rates

The currency converter and the “≈ in your currency” figures use official central bank exchange rates. To get them, BudT requests the rates from Frankfurter, an open-source service that republishes rates from central banks.

A request contains only:

  • the currency codes involved, for example NGN and USD
  • the central bank being asked

Like any internet request, it also reveals your device’s IP address to that service. It contains nothing you’ve entered in BudT. Rates are saved on your device so the converter works offline.

If you tap a link to a central bank’s website, it opens in your browser, and that website’s own privacy policy applies.

Backups and exports

When you choose Back up data or Export transactions, BudT creates a file and opens your device’s share sheet. You decide where the file goes, for example:

  • your Files app
  • a cloud drive
  • email

Once shared, the file is handled by the service you choose under that service’s terms.

The backup file is not encrypted, so store it somewhere you trust. Restore from backup only reads the file you pick.

Device backups

Your phone’s own backup features, such as Android’s automatic backup to Google Drive or iCloud backups on iPhone, may include BudT’s data, depending on your device settings. These backups are managed by Google or Apple, not by us.

Deleting your data

To delete your data:

  • Use Settings → Erase all data in the app, or uninstall BudT. Both delete the data from your device.
  • Delete any backup or export files you saved separately.

Children

BudT is not directed at children under 13. It doesn’t collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Financial information disclaimer

Calculator results and exchange rates are estimates for information only, not financial, tax or legal advice.

Changes to this policy

If BudT starts handling data differently, for example by adding optional cloud sync or in-app purchases, we’ll update this page and the effective date before the change reaches the app.
